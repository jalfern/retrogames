# COMET DRIFT GP — engine notes (#419, polished #435, course pack #493)

Original arcade kart racer. Course pack: SUNSET GULCH (dusk canyon),
SANDBANK SPEED BOWL (noon, Daytona road-course spirit) and CORKSCOMB DUNES
(dawn, Laguna Seca spirit), 4 laps,
7 buggies (player + 6 CPU). No items — the drift ladder is the whole game
economy. ←/→ on the attract screen picks the circuit; the label rides in the
HUD; the choice persists in localStorage (`retrogames:comet.course`) and HD
follows it. All art/audio synthesized at runtime; no assets.

**Course pick is touch + stick (#534):** the attract's ‹ › chevrons are
tappable (a phone has no stick on the title) — tapping one cycles the circuit
and does NOT start; a tap anywhere else (the name, prompt, sky) still races
the pick. The analog stick and a gamepad's left stick cycle through the shared
`padState.x` (edge-triggered, re-arms at centre: one flick = one step). The
chevron geometry + hit test live in `selector.js` (pure — the renderer, the
pointer handler and `cometcheck` all audit the same targets).

## Layout

- `reference.js` (#513 redo) — GENERATED by `scripts/extract-comet-references.mjs`,
  traced from the REAL published maps committed under `data/maps/`:
  **daytona-road-course.png** (Wikimedia "Daytona International Speedway - Road
  Course.png", public domain) and **Laguna_Seca.svg** (Wikimedia, CC BY-SA 3.0).
  The generator Chrome-rasterises the map, masks bright pixels (road lines + grey
  apron), closes gaps, downsamples 3x, Zhang-Suen thins and walks the longest
  closed loop; `normalize`/`resample`/`shapeDistance`/`bestShapeDistance` are pure
  so the harness can grade shape. `bestShapeDistance` takes the better of direct
  and mirror-x alignment (both current layouts match DIRECT — the maps as drawn).
- `track.js` — the COURSE PACK. (#513) the world grew to **2560²** and the asphalt
  **narrowed again** (`ROAD_R 140→80→64`): a real hairpin / Corkscrew / Bus Stop is
  a braking corner whose own two road legs cannot fit inside the old 280 px canyon;
  64 keeps the traced maps' closest leg pairs clear. `COURSES`
  (gulch, sandbank, corkscomb), each a closed Catmull-Rom centerline, plus a live
  `TRACK`/`SCENERY`/`CHECKPOINTS` view of the SELECTED course — `selectCourse(id)`
  swaps contents in place. **SANDBANK control points sit on the traced Daytona road
  course** (front straight → Turns 1-2 → back straight → International Horseshoe →
  T5-7 kinks → Bus Stop) and **CORKSCOMB on the traced Laguna Seca** (Rahal
  straight → Andretti hook → Corkscomb double-apex → Snake → keyhole), then
  arc-weighted spread + corner-rounding make the tight sections self-clearing at
  the 60 px radius floor (the 220 oval-only floor was dropped in #513 — hairpins
  are real braking corners now). SUNSET GULCH keeps its original data.
  Reference maps credited: Daytona PNG public domain, Laguna Seca CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `sim.js` — the pure machine (`makeGame(seed)`, `step`, `decide`,
  `stateHash`). Determinism: mulberry32 riding INSIDE `g.rngState` (JSON-safe
  snapshots); `Math.random` is never called. Launch jitter, the seeded rival
  mistakes (wide-exit 72% / spin-out otherwise, skill-weighted) and the
  slipstream (a kart 55–190 px dead ahead, |lat|<46, adds 0.02/tick) all read
  as pure position math. The drift: hold + steer above v 4.5 charges
  50/105/165 ticks; releasing pays `TIERS[t].dur` of over-cap boost; the
  player's Comet engine multiplies duration ×1.45. Drift steer AUTHORITY decays
  with `driftT` and the yaw rate is capped (`driftYawCap`/`driftDecayT`/
  `driftAuthFloor`, #440): a held drift flicks in, carves a fixed radius, then
  straightens — so `driftT` still climbs the ladder and pays gold, but a drift
  held on a straight can no longer wind the kart into a spin. Rivals and the player share
  `drive()`; `brain()` is shared by rivals (their skill) and the autopilot
  (`decide`, skill 1.0). `decide()` never makes mistakes — it is the certified
  line the harness and the attract demo replay.
- `art.js` — `PALETTE` (named dusk-canyon roles: strata, apron, worn asphalt,
  mesas) + `PALETTES`/`paletteFor` (#493: `noon` and `dawn` re-tint every
  role — noon: blue-white sky, pale sand, grey-slate asphalt; dawn:
  indigo-to-amber first light, rose strata, long shadows; the same named
  roles keep cometcheck's look-pins honest per course), `LIVERY` (per driver), `buildGround` (concentric strata strokes
  widest-first foot-to-rim, sand apron, worn wheel grooves via offset paths,
  deterministic patches + sealed cross-cracks, crumbled sandstone edge rings —
  NO painted kerb, NO centre dash: the canyon look is pinned by cometcheck),
  `MESA_FAR`/`MESA_NEAR` + `longestFlatRun` (pure flat-topped mesa profiles),
  `buildSky` (two parallax mesa bands + setting sun + ember glint),
  `buildSprites` (exposed-wheel buggies: 3 steer poses × 2 rev frames),
  `minimapPath` (HUD outline + live dot projection). Node-safe on import;
   `document` only inside builders.
- `font.js` (#440 polish 3) — the game's OWN 3×5 bitmap face (`FONT`,
   `drawText`/`textW`): rounded numerals + full uppercase + HUD symbols, every
   glyph generated in code. The canvas HUD used to lean on the browser default
   `bold Npx monospace`; `index.jsx` now routes EVERY canvas glyph through
   `drawText` (baseline semantics preserved, so layout coords survive), so the
   racer draws with a designed arcade font instead of a per-OS fallback.
    `cometcheck`'s own-font gate proves it and kills the system-font/glyph mutants.
 - Rival boost sfx (#440 polish 2 item 5) — the sim announces EVERY kart's
    drift release (`{e:'boost', who}`), but the sfx loop used to react to
    COMET only, so the pack's gold releases were silent. `sim.js` now exports
    the pure loudness law `rivalBoostFx(dist, tier)`: quieter than the player's
    own boost at the same tier (max 0.07 vs 0.11), linear fade over the gap,
    silent past 700 units, gold louder than blue, `far` flips the timbre to a
    soft triangle. `index.jsx` plays the two nearest releases per tick so a
     three-wide pack never stacks; cometcheck's rival-sfx gates kill the flat-law,
     too-loud and silent-pack mutants.
  - Overtake readability (#440 polish 2 item 2) — at contact distance a rival
     buggy grew past half the portrait screen (measured 65–160% of screen height
     at f 30..90) and hid the road ahead. `art.js` exports the pure law
     `rivalViewFx(pxH, H)`: a rival covering > 25% of screen height darkens
     (floor 0.55 alpha), past 55% it shrinks to cap; far rivals render
     untouched. `index.jsx` routes rival sprites (and their shadows) through it
     — the player kart and scenery never damp. cometcheck's readability gates
      kill the no-shrink, flat-alpha and swallowed-threshold mutants.
   - Engine voice (#440 polish 2 item 6) — the bed used to be one square buzz
      whose pop period stepped with `Math.round(v)` (a plastic gear-step at every
      integer of speed), dead-steady pitch, drifts and sand silent bar the
      one-shot crashes. `engineSfx.js` exports the pure laws: `engineVoice`
      (continuous period `max(3, 15−v)`, pitch riding 58+13·v plus the old
      34+52·tier boost ladder, wobbling on two incommensurate sines ±7.5 Hz),
      `driftScrape` (filtered-noise crackle under held drifts, swelling and
      darkening 2400→1500 Hz as the tyre charges), `sandWhoosh` (low filtered
      bed off-road, 300–800 Hz — under the scrape floor, since sand drinks the
      top end). `index.jsx` only accumulates phases and calls AudioController;
      cometcheck's engine-voice gates kill the gear-step, dead-pitch,
      straight-wheel-scrape, glass-sand and bypass mutants.
 - `index.jsx` — the shell: fixed-timestep loop (`contractHoldsLoop` gates the
  sim, the contract owns the clock while held), adaptive-source Mode 7 raster
  (the canvas re-solves to the viewport aspect: height-anchored
  176×≤384 landscape, width-anchored 256×≤448 portrait — the race fills the
  phone instead of sitting in it), low chase camera (CAM_H 46, horizon 0.4H)
  with a per-tier boost FOV punch and drift camera-lag, carved HUD cluster
  mounted in the SKY band (POS badge, speed bar + chevron drift ladder,
  centre LAP plate, minimap — the road view is never covered), sandstone
  start arch (no gantry), renderer-only fx (tier sparks, apron dust on every
  held drift/offroad run, edge speed-streaks, tiered tunnel vignette), HUD
  typography scales with `hs = min(H/176, W/256)` so portrait never explodes,
  attract = autopilot race, countdown, end table, localStorage best/top-3,
  DEV hooks (`__cometTest` incl. `view()`, `__cometLog`, DebugKit F1) and the
  game contract (`exposeGame`, id `comet-drift`, snapshot/restore included).

## Feel tuning (measured, not vibes)

- `top 8.6` px/tick ≈ 168 km/h; kerb 7.2 (kerb removed — keep the constant
  for the sand/road ladder), sand 3.4; steer 0.052 with grip `min(1, v/2.2)`;
  drift yaw ×1.6 with a 0.35π lateral slide.
- Course min radius 288 px → flat-out everywhere on the ideal line; corners
  are won on the drift ladder, not on braking.
- #518 elevation: every course carries a closed height profile (`ELEV` anchors,
  [lap-fraction, px], resampled to a uniform knot grid then periodic
  Catmull-Rom; h=0 at the start/finish, auto-capped at `ELEV_MAX_GRADE` 0.095
  so authored amplitudes stay arcade-steep — gulch 56px, sandbank 125px,
  corkscomb 186px of relief). The sim's only slope reading is `p.grade`
  (dh/ds): `k.v -= CFG.slopeK · grade · cos(heading − trackHeading)`
  (slopeK 0.42) — downhill gains, uphill pays, no jumps/airtime. 2D stays
  flat (Mode 7); crests/dips are faked with a smoothed ±6px horizon bob
  driven by grade+height.
- Rival skills 0.785–0.86 (top-speed multipliers) + defence shuffle
  (inside-line pull when a chaser is within 460 px) + slipstream + seeded
  mistakes — the pack stays close enough to see and use. The autopilot
  (skill 1.0 + Comet turbo) wins from P7 on every seed tested — the field is
  beatable, not a procession: check `cometcheck`.
- Speed presentation: edge streaks above 7.6 (denser + tinted on boost), FOV
  punch `0.05+0.035·tier` lerped at 0.08, camera kick 4+3·tier px, engine
  tone steps up per tier.
- #426 pack-read slice: the sim sets `k.braking` every tick it brakes (renderer
  paints twin red tail-lamps + halo on karts, player and field) and emits
  `{e:'tier', who, tier}` when a charge crosses each TIERS threshold upward —
  the player's crossing pays a rising chirp (violet/gold now audible, not just
  the release sweep). Also fixed: sfx/best-lap handlers compared `ev.who ===
  'PLAYER'` but events carry `k.name` ('COMET') — spin/bump/lap sounds and the
  localStorage best-lap table had been dead since the rename; and the
  cometcheck infield-cut probe throttles now (#426) — on the grown world it
  coasted short of the line, so its `if (crossed)` mutant survived (blind) on
  main @ c5eda5f; with the throttle the gate is load-bearing again.

## Honest gaps (filed to QA as the next polish pass)

- The schematic maps put some parallel roads closer together than two roads of
  any sane width can sit (Daytona's fork legs, Laguna's keyhole). The re-tune
  spread those gaps to the minimum self-clearing distance instead of deleting
  them; the worst local deviation from the traced line is ~10% (sandbank) and
  ~17% (corkscomb) and cometcheck caps them there. A taller world or narrower
  lane could tighten the caps further.
- Controls per se are #431 (steering curve); this pass kept the sim's numbers.
  Measured (cometplay + Node trace): keyboard steer is raw ±1, so a
  one-direction held drift walks the kart off the asphalt in ~20 ticks at any
  speed — tier 1 of the ladder is UNREACHABLE by holding one way. The ladder
  is a zigzag game (alternate steering ~8 ticks while Space holds); cometplay
  drives and grades exactly that. A softer keyboard steer notch would change
  the feel — a #431 decision, not a harness one. #517 (Jon's call, 2026-10-21)
  teaches the technique instead of bending the controls: the sim flags the
  player's first off-road drift-dump that pays nothing (release while
  off-road with charge < tier 1) as a once-per-race `zigHint` event + a
  240-tick countdown, and the HUD draws "OFF-ROAD! TAP-TAP THE STEER" in
  the sky band while it runs. Gates: cometcheck drives the measured repro
  (held one-way drift at a Corkscomb bend) and three mutants — silence
  (hintT=0), spam (guard removed), permanent HUD (decay removed).
- #493 ships TWO new courses (Sandbank, Corkscomb) per the issue's ship-small
  plan; Dry Lake Bed (~Sebring) remains the optional third. At flat-world scale
  and the 220px radius floor the Corkscomb reads as a rate-alternating crest
  complex, not the hill-drop double-S of the real thing — a longer-wavelength
  re-tune is polish-pass territory. HD keeps its dusk sky tint on any circuit
  (noon/dawn looks are 2D-only so far); the shared trackside arch design is
  per-name, not per-character (Sandbank and Corkscomb deserve their own gantry
  shapes in the polish pass).
- No cups, items, or real music (the engine bed is a synthesized voice with
  scrape/whoosh layers since #440 item 6, but there is no music track) —
  music out of scope per #419.
- CPU karts never chain multi-tier drifts deliberately; they hold one bend
  and bank whatever tier they reach.
- Sand and cliff are surfaces + clamp; no dynamic deformation.
- The strata are texture bands, not true vertical geometry (Mode 7 limitation
  accepted — the Road Rash trick was always fake).
- Banking cue (#502 item 4): `bankAt(i)` in track.js is the shared measure
  (smoothed |curvature| / BANK_K); `bankRimDefs()` paints dark rim-shadow dots
  on the outside of banked bends in the ground bake, so Sandbank's bowl leans
  before the tyres do. Gated in cometcheck.
