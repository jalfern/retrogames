PLAYER TWO WANTED!

HEY, FRIEND OF JON!

Jon is training an AI that plays arcade games with people, and listens when a teammate yells “grab the key!” It can only learn from real humans playing for real. The bot's never met you. Grab your phone, play a few rounds, and talk while you do. Your clumsy runs teach it as much as your great ones.

YOUR KIT

What you'll need

A phone (Safari or Chrome)
Your voice
About 20 minutes
Wi-Fi or data

No account, no app to install. It's a web page.

STEP 1

Open the arcade

Reading this on a computer? Point your phone camera at the square. On your phone already? Tap the link.

jalfern.com/retrogames/gauntlet
Turn the phone sideways!
STEP 2

The “Record this player?” screen

Tap to start. Before the first run, the game asks if it can record you. Nothing is switched on until you switch it on.

NO THANKS
  1. Type your name. Use the same name every time, on every game. That's how the bot knows all your runs are one person.
  2. Flip both switches green. The voice one matters most.

MEANWHILE…
Sharing a phone with someone?
Each person gets their own name and their own answer. Never play under someone else's name!
NO TWINS!
STEP 3

Play Gauntlet… and talk the whole time

MIC ON · LISTENING
ABOW

You're the yellow hero. A blue CPU partner follows you through the maze. Left thumb walks, A swings your sword, BOW shoots arrows. Find keys, open doors, eat food when your health gets low.

Now the important bit: tap the MIC pill in the corner and boss your partner around. It actually listens. Try these:

  • “Follow me!”
  • “Grab the key!”
  • “Hold here!”
  • “Take the upper right!”

Then just keep talking like you would to a friend on the couch: “ugh, ghosts”, “wait, where's the door?”, “nope nope nope.” That out-loud chatter is exactly what the bot needs to hear.

STEP 4

Watch for the badge

When a run ends, a little badge pops up. Either one means it worked:

✓ TAKE UPLOADED
⏳ TAKE SAVED — WILL SYNC

“Will sync” means your signal was weak. It sends the next time you open the arcade with a connection, so open the link again later.

THE BOT SAYS…
Don't try to play perfectly!
I learn from the mistakes, the panic and the “wait, go back!” moments too.
And play solo with the CPU partner. Online rooms with friends aren't recorded yet.
STEP 5

Then play a second game

The bot has to learn more than one game, so play at least one of these too. Same name, both switches on. Find the mic button and talk here too.

★ START HERE SHORT ORDER SHOUT

An all-night diner. Burgers, soup and burner fires, with the CPU cook across the counter. Hold the MIC button and yell orders.

SWEATY PALMS BOMB DEFUSE

Two hands, one bomb. Both of you turn the keys together or nothing counts. Try “cut blue!” and “hold!”

MUDDY RALLY CO-DRIVER

Drive blind on a rally stage. The pace notes are all you know. Read them out loud: “left two!”, “hard right!”

MISSION CARD

Mission complete when…

  • You played Gauntlet a few times (3 or more is great)
  • You played at least one other game from Step 5 a couple of times
  • Voice was on, and you talked
  • You used the same name every time
  • You texted Jon your name so he can thank you
THE FINE PRINT (IT'S SHORT)
What's recorded
Your button presses and touches, the game's random seed and checkpoints so the run can be replayed, and your mic audio, but only if you turned the voice switch on.
Where it goes
A private research store that only Jon can see. It's used to train his game bot and nothing else.
Saying no
Tap NO THANKS and the game plays exactly the same. Nothing leaves the phone.
Changing your mind
Your answer stays on that phone for 90 days, then it asks again. Want your recordings deleted? Tell Jon and he'll remove them.
