VERDICT (first pass, cap 1024 — SUPERSEDED by the fair-horizon rescore)

The Astra recipe did not reproduce with our budget and our localization. Removing our loop-write direction from layers 15/19 — even from all columns (0.98% of weights) — left 12/12 practice problems non-terminating; the ablated model stayed coherent but rambled longer. Within a 1024-token horizon, 9,220 "looping" positions look like verbose self-questioning, not the tight attractor MIT traced over 32k-token horizons.

The control row caught us out. An early run looked like the model-card fix (presence penalty 2.0) suddenly terminating 12/12 — too good. It was a harness artifact: the override flag was appended after the standard 1.5, so the command carried --presence-penalty 1.5 --presence-penalty 2.0. With the override applied cleanly (single flag), termination collapsed to 0/12.

THE BUG — and the cap that hid it

Qwen's own model card (L856) warns Qwen3.5-0.8B falls into thinking loops. The first pass measured at a 1024-token cap: 36/36 train problems hit the cap — including "What is 17×23?". But the cap itself was the confound (Jon's steer, #1493): at cap 4096, penalty-free, the same base model finishes:

runfinishedcap-hitloop-flaggedcorrect

So: at 4096, ? finishes and only ? hits the cap — of which only ? carries the exact-repeat loop signature. The non-termination at 1024 was mostly truncation of long thinking, not the MIT loop.

THE FIX — our own localization, all layers, per layer

MIT CSAIL (Takehi et al., Nov 2026) fixed Qwen3.5-0.8B by subtracting one direction written by the MLPs of layers 15 & 19 from the top down_proj columns — 0.016% of the weights — at a horizon of 32,768 tokens. Per the steer, we scanned all 24 layers for the loop-vs-finished MLP-write contrast (no 15/19 prior; fixes the layer-pooling shortcut in the first-pass capture), ranked separability per layer, and removed the per-layer direction from the top-K columns of the highest-ranked layers:
