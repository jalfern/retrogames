SHORE OPEN ▸

A WebGPU golden-hour beach: a 2D shallow-water sim runs the swash on the GPU (wetting and drying the sand, lateral momentum, real run-up and backwash), swell refracts across the bar by an eikonal travel-time field, and foam is injected at the breakers then advected and decayed as a persistent field — lace left on drying sand. Two-stream water colour, mirror-wet sand and a ported ocean sky. Drag to look around; WASD + Q/E to fly.

STORM OPEN ▸

A WebGL2 thunderstorm rolling over an open field: a lobed cumulonimbus raymarched with sun-occlusion lighting — flat dark wall base, sunlit flanks, an anvil deck — behind two parallax slabs of driving rain. BLACK HOLE OPEN ▸

A slow flight past an event horizon in WebGL2: every pixel is a null geodesic integrated around a Schwarzschild hole, so the starfield bends and the thin Keplerian accretion disk wraps over and under the shadow — the Interstellar halo. The approaching side of the disk runs brighter and bluer by relativistic Doppler beaming; a thin photon ring rims the black. Drag to look around; click for a synthesized gravitational drone.
