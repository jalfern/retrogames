# FROSTBRUSH (`fox-gemini`) — Design Note

**FROSTBRUSH** (`public/demos/fox-gemini.html`) is a self-contained, zero-library raw WebGL2 demo of a procedural 3D red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*) running a seamless rotary double-suspension gallop down a frosted boreal silver-birch glade at dawn. Every part of the fox—supple continuous spine, white-bibbed chest ruff, diamond cheek flares, slender rostrum and glossy nose button, slanted amber eyes, tall black-backed ears, digitigrade black-stockinged limbs, and 50 cm snow-tipped brush—is lofted procedurally on CPU and lit with a real 1024² PCF directional sun shadow map, guard-hair rim sheen, four-toed frost pawprints, breath mist plumes, and synthesized paw-pad + hoarfrost audio.

## Five Signature Features

1. **True Carnivore Rotary Double-Suspension Gallop (`LH → RH → RF → LF`):** Unlike equine transverse gallops, the fox runs a cursorial carnivore rotary sequence with **two** airborne flight phases per stride—an **extended flight** (`~19%` of stride, spine arched open, forepaws reaching 84 cm ahead of hindpaws) after hind pushoff, and a **gathered coiled flight** (`~25%` of stride, lumbar spine flexed upward, wider-straddling hindpaws crossing ahead of forepaws under the chest) after fore pushoff.
2. **Exact Zero-Slip World-Pinned Stance & Seamless 12-Stride World Loop:** Every stance paw is algebraically locked to its world touchdown anchor (`0.000 mm` slip across analytic sweeps and live rAF frames) and connected by $C^1$-continuous Hermite swing trajectories (`0` world velocity at liftoff and touchdown) over an 18.0 m (12-stride) periodic terrain and birch glade (`0.000 mm` loop seam).
3. **Seamless Continuous Spine Lofting & Digitigrade IK:** A single 15-station lofted tube spans rump-to-nose without neck seams while analytical 2-bone IK drives backwards-hinging elbows, compliant carpi, forwards-hinging stifles, and high backwards-jutting calcaneal hocks strictly within bone reach (`maxIkRatio < 0.98`).
4. **Raw WebGL2 Boreal Dawn Glade & Real PCF Shadow Map:** Depth-texture sun shadows (`DEPTH_COMPONENT24` + 3×3 PCF), wrap-Lambert + sky-hemisphere lighting, backlit golden guard-hair rim sheen, silver birches with dark bark lenticels and autumn-gold canopies, hoarfrost turf, drifting birch leaves, frost kick-up clods, and stride-coupled warm breath plumes.
5. **Interactive Gait Study Camera & Synthesized Frost Audio:** Drag-to-orbit camera, 4 view presets (3/4 track, Muybridge side study, low brush chase, overhead rotary footfall view), slow-motion study toggle, tempo control, and Web Audio cushioned paw-pad thuds + hoarfrost crunch + dawn breeze backed by `window.__foxTest`.
