# SUNSTRIDE (`horse-gemini`) — Design Note

**SUNSTRIDE** (`public/demos/horse-gemini.html`) is a self-contained, zero-library WebGL2 procedural low-poly 3D demo of a bay mustang running in a seamless four-beat transverse gallop across a golden-hour red-mesa steppe. Every anatomical part—crested neck, sculpted equine head with ganache jowl, muzzle and swiveling ears, deep barrel ribcage, rising withers and rounded croup, gliding scapulae, backwards-folding fore-knees (carpi), forwards-stifle / backwards-hock hindlimbs, compliant pasterns, hooves, and flowing multi-segment mane and tail—is built procedurally from flat-shaded faceted primitives with analytical face normals, directional sunlight, hemisphere sky/turf bounce, projected ground silhouette shadow, dust puffs, and synthesized four-beat turf audio.

## Five Signature Features

1. **Exact Zero-Slip World-Pinned Stance + Seamless Periodic Loop:** Each hoof runs a right-lead transverse gallop (`LH → RH → LF → RF → gathered suspension`) whose stance trajectory is algebraically locked to its world touchdown coordinate (`0.000 mm` ground slip) while matching ground retraction speed (`C¹` continuous liftoff/touchdown) so `pose(0) === pose(1)` with zero seam.
2. **True Equine Limb Kinematics & Reciprocal Apparatus:** Analytical 2-bone IK drives 6-part forelimbs (gliding scapula, humerus, forearm, backwards-hinging carpus, cannon, hyperextending fetlock/pastern, hoof) and hindlimbs (thigh, forward-bending stifle, gaskin, high backwards-opening hock, cannon, pastern, hoof) so every stride phase reads anatomically as a horse rather than a generic quadruped.
3. **Whole-Body Gallop Bascule & Secondary Motion:** Trunk pitch and heave launch upward off the hind drive and vault over the forelegs into collected flight, coupled 1:1 with barrel breathing, counter-nodding neck/head bascule, ear flicks, and traveling-wave inertia down a multi-segment black mane, forelock, and streaming S-curve tail.
4. **Golden-Hour Faceted Low-Poly Scene in Raw WebGL2:** Flat-shaded copper-bay coat with dark stockings, white hind socks, and forehead blaze lit by warm low sun, cool sky fill, rim highlights, a real-time planar projected horse shadow, persistent horseshoe prints, turf dust clods, sagebrush, and distant layered mesas tiled at an integer multiple of stride length.
5. **Interactive Muybridge Study Camera & Synthesized 4-Beat Audio:** Orbit drag-to-look, camera angle presets, smooth tempo control, a slow-motion study toggle, and Web Audio hoof-strike synthesis (`da-da-da-dum` turf thump + grit + prairie wind, unlocked on first gesture) backed by `window.__horsegeminiTest` (`ready`, `frames`, `setPose`, gait & slip sensors).
