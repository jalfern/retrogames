# THOROUGHBRED (`horse-gemini-hd`) — Design Note

**THOROUGHBRED** (`public/demos/horse-gemini-hd.html`) is a self-contained, zero-library HD WebGL2 showcase of an athletic bay thoroughbred galloping in a seamless loop down a sunlit highland meadow track framed by wind-rippled fescue grass and misted mountain ridges. Moving beyond faceted low-poly primitives, the entire axial equine body—from flared nostrils, muzzle, mandibular ganache jowl, supraorbital brow, and cupped ears, through the narrow throatlatch, jugular-grooved arched neck, prominent withers, deep pectoral brisket, breathing ribcage, tucked inguinal flank, and cleft gluteal croup—is lofted as one continuous smooth-shaded spline surface with Fourier anatomical cross-sections, dynamic stride-driven muscle bulge, and root-normal-blended limbs sculpted down to the olecranon elbow, pisiform carpus, Achilles-tendon calcaneal hock, dry flexor-grooved cannons, compliant fetlocks, and bell-walled hooves, lit by a 2048² PCF shadow map, physically-inspired velvet/satin coat sheen, Kajiya-Kay anisotropic horsehair, and ACES-tonemapped bloom.

## Five Signature Features

1. **Continuous Smooth-Lofted Equine Anatomy & Dynamic Muscle Shape:** A single welded Catmull-Rom axial mesh (`muzzle → head → neck → withers → barrel → flank → croup`) paired with four continuous high-resolution limb skins whose shoulder and haunch skirts transfer surface normals seamlessly into the trunk, featuring sculpted scapular/triceps and gluteal/gaskin muscle bellies that flex under stance load, dry tendon-grooved cannons, cupped pinna ears, glossy corneal eyes, and breathing-dilated nostrils.
2. **Exact Zero-Slip Transverse Gallop with Terminal Retraction & Toe Breakover:** Right-lead four-beat transverse gallop (`LH → RH → LF → RF → gathered suspension`) where terminal swing retraction matches horizontal ground speed at touchdown, stance hooves lock algebraically to exact world coordinates (`0.000 mm` slip) while fetlocks hyperextend under vertical load and heels peel around the pinned toe at breakover, and `pose(0) === pose(1)` loops seamlessly with analytical 2-bone IK.
3. **Whole-Body Bascule & Multi-Ribbon Inertial Mane, Forelock, and Tail:** Hind-drive pitch-up, forehand vault, spine collection during gathered flight, and counter-nodding neck/head bascule drive 24 curved 3D mane/forelock ribbons and an 18-plume wind-streamed tail with phase-lagged traveling whip waves, lateral splay, and ear swivels.
4. **Multi-Pass WebGL2 Shadow-Mapped Coat, Hair & Highland Scene Pipeline:** Real-time 2048² PCF soft directional shadow mapping (casting horse self-shadows and ground shadows), dual-lobe satin + grazing-velvet equine coat shading (with 4 selectable coats), anisotropic Kajiya-Kay hair highlights, Rayleigh/Mie sky with drifting cirrus, GPU-instanced wind-swaying grass and wildflowers, normal-indented damp horseshoe prints, backlit turf clods, and ACES bloom—all zero-GC and mobile-tuned for 60 fps on phones.
5. **Interactive Muybridge Study & Synthesized Four-Beat Audio:** Drag-to-orbit and pinch-to-zoom camera, four cinematic angle presets (`1–4`), slow-motion Muybridge study mode (`SPACE`), gallop tempo (`↑/↓`), coat cycling (`C`), and Web Audio procedural turf thuds, gravel crunch, nostril exhalations, and valley wind unlocked on first gesture, verified end-to-end by `window.__horsegeminihdTest` and `scripts/horsegeminihdcheck.mjs`.
